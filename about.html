Our Philosophy

Your Health Is Our Priority

Located at Rodi Road, Hafzwala, Piplan, Punjab, Al Rahman Medical Store was established with a singular mission: to ensure every patient in our community has instant access to 100% genuine, unadulterated pharmaceutical supplies and healthcare products.

Our Rigorous Quality Verification

We source directly from licensed pharmaceutical distributors and manufacturers. Every batch of medication undergoes strict inspection for batch numbers, expiry verification, and seal integrity before being placed in our temperature-controlled facility.

Cold-Chain Excellence

Dedicated refrigeration ensures insulin, vaccines, and bio-therapeutics retain 100% potency.

Community First

24/7 emergency response and home delivery to support vulnerable patients across Piplan.
